THE JOURNEY DOESN'T END WITH ALZHEIMER'S

Memory Lane provides affordable, guided local outings for seniors with Alzheimer's across the Lower Mainland. Through thoughtfully planned experiences, we support their physical and cognitive well-being while bringing more connection and enrichment into their everyday lives.

This project was created in collaboration with Shilo Sun, who was responsible for all illustrations.
View Case Study

Through nostalgic illustrations, warm colours, and simple layouts, Memory Lane welcomes families to its local outing programs with clarity and ease.

We recognize the stress that can follow a loved one’s diagnosis and the emotional weight of watching them change over time. Our goal is to make finding support feel simple and reassuring, offering families and their loved ones a moment of relief and the opportunity to create new memories, even as old ones begin to fade.

Each senior receives a travel kit containing mini brochures and a trackable name tag all tucked away in a fanny pack. The kit encourages independence while helping caregivers and loved ones keep track of seniors throughout their outings.

Designed with seniors’ visual needs in mind, each brochure features large, legible text and simple illustrations that introduce the day’s destination and activities. These accessible guides help seniors stay informed and engaged during their trip.

Through Memory Lane, we hope to help seniors continue finding joy, connection, and enrichment beyond their diagnosis while showing everyone that the journey doesn't end with Alzheimer's.
