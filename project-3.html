WHIPPING NOSTALGIA INTO EVERY BITE

    CLIENT PROJECT
    VISUAL UPDATE
    SOCIAL MEDIA MARKETTING

Tochi Desserts Ltd. is a Vancouver-based dessert company serving up fun, creativity, and nostalgia in every bite. Best known for its signature Mini Mochi HK-Style French Toast, Tochi brings a playful twist to familiar Asian-inspired treats. What started as a pop-up at the Richmond Night Market in 2023 has since grown into a food truck, expanding its menu with the Tochi Sando, a cream-filled Japanese dessert sandwich.

The original brand identity for Tochi Desserts Ltd. was developed by Jasmine Chen. The work presented reflects my contributions to the brand's creative direction and development. Some designs may differ from their final implementation.

THE CHALLENGE

What began as a side project between two university students quickly grew into a full-fledged business, leaving little time to focus on the brand's development. The goal was to refresh Tochi's existing identity to better reflect its youthful, playful energy while creating a consistent and recognizable experience across its food truck and social media presence.

REKINDLING INDEPENDENCE

With their travel kits in hand, seniors can enjoy the freedom to explore and make new memories, while their caregivers enjoy a well-deserved moment of rest. Each trip is designed to be safe, accessible, and low-stimulation, giving families peace of mind while providing seniors with meaningful moments outside of their daily routine
